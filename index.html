Season 5.0 Flagship Attractions (22–24 January 2027)

Hosted at Indore's iconic heritage landmark, Historic Gandhi Hall, M.G. Road, organized by Kalastambh (Founder: Sapna Katfar | Director: Pushkar Soni).

1. National Fine Arts & Photography Exhibition

Showcase paintings, sculptures, digital installations, tribal Gond & Bhil art, and master photography portfolios across 300+ curated walls.

2. Sustainable Handloom Fashion Show (23 Jan 2027)

Premier design & fashion colleges present upcycled couture and Madhya Pradesh handloom collections on a 40-ft heritage catwalk.

3. Grand Unveiling of 12 × 16 ft 'E-Tiger' (22 Jan 2027)

Curtain-drop unveiling of the monumental E-Tiger installation honoring Tiger State Madhya Pradesh and Swachh Indore.
