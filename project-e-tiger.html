Project E-Tiger Movement

A statewide environmental awareness and youth participation movement mobilizing 1,00,000 citizens and 1,000 E-Tiger Youth Ambassadors to channelize 95%+ collected electronic waste to CPCB/MPPCB-authorized recyclers and unveil a monumental 12 × 16 ft E-Tiger installation at Historic Gandhi Hall, Indore on 22 January 2027.
